If Axle has called an event

The charge percentage above does not change. What changes is how much you stop the battery selling during the 16:00–19:00 peak, because after that it is normally empty and you can only import at 6 kW.

On a bright day the battery often still holds 2–5 kWh at 19:00, so you may need less than this. Holding 6 kWh back costs about £1.63 of lost peak export against an event paying about £6 — worth it on a called day, never as a standing habit.

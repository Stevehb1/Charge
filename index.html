If Axle has called an event

The overnight charge percentage above does not change. Two things do.

1. Have the battery at 40% when the event starts — or 75% for a two-hour event. That is the deliverable energy it needs at 6 kW.

2. Stop the 16:00–19:00 peak taking it below this floor, because after 19:00 you can only refill at 6 kW:

“None” means let the peak run normally — there is time to import the lot afterwards. On a bright day the battery often still holds 2–5 kWh at 19:00, so the floor can be lower than shown. Holding 6 kWh back costs about £1.63 of lost peak export against an event paying about £6 — worth it on a called day, never as a standing habit.
